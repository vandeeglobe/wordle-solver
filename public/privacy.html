Privacy

This page describes how the site behaves. It is not legal advice.

Guesses you type

Letters and colors stay in the browser while this page is open. Reset, or reloading the page, clears them. The site has no accounts and does not send those guesses to a server of its own.

Advertising

The page loads Google AdSense for publisher ca-pub-1543476548216945. Google may use cookies and similar data to choose and measure ads. Google describes that in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Hosting

The site is hosted on Vercel. When someone opens a page, Vercel receives ordinary connection details such as the IP address and browser. Vercel describes its handling of that data in the Vercel privacy policy.

What the site does not ask for

There is no signup, payment, or form that asks for a name or email address.
